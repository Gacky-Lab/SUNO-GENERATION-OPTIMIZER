# P2 separate one-factor intervention (registered after pilot v1, before intervention)

Pilot v1 did not trigger the all-fail rule because B1 contained one acceptable provider candidate and one 136 BPM outlier. Do not retrospectively change v1 or merge this intervention into its A/B result.

Separate exploratory mechanism test: take the known B1 provider tempo mismatch as a trigger for an explicitly human-authorized one-factor change from style_influence 0.4 to 0.5. Keep lyrics, style, v6, variety, duration and evaluation endpoints as in v1. One standard generation only, 10 extra song credits maximum, no downloads. New title and idempotency key. Compare both results descriptively to B1 (136.0,178.7 BPM) and all eight fixed-control candidates. This does not prove efficacy: regression to the mean, selection on failure, stochastic variation and provider measurement error are uncontrolled. Independent quality gate remains UNKNOWN.
